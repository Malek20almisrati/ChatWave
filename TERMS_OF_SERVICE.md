## 1. The service and acceptance

ChatWave is planned as a social communication service for profiles, posts, photos, videos, channels, stories, comments, groups, and private messages. By creating an account or using the live service, you agree to these terms, the [Privacy Notice](PRIVACY_POLICY.md), the [Community and Child Safety Policy](CHILD_SAFETY_POLICY.md), and any feature-specific terms shown to you. If you do not agree, do not use the live service. Mandatory consumer rights under local law are not waived.

This project includes a browser preview and an optional local development API. The documented local server can create development accounts, store text posts and messages in a local database, and receive files into private quarantine storage. Local OTP codes are printed to the server terminal. No public account service, external SMS delivery, or production moderation operation is configured here. A local development code is not production identity or age verification.

## 2. Eligibility, age, and guardians

You may use the live service only if it is lawful for you to do so in your country and you meet the applicable minimum age and consent requirements. You must provide accurate age information. Where a parent or guardian’s verifiable consent is required, an account cannot collect or use the child’s personal information until that consent has been obtained through the approved process. A checkbox is not a substitute for required verification. Parents/guardians may contact `[guardian-support contact]` to review, correct, or request deletion of a child’s information, subject to applicable law.

The service will apply age-appropriate privacy, discovery, messaging, recommendation, and content controls. Local rules differ; eligibility and features may therefore vary by country. The prototype currently routes people under 18 to a protected preview because it cannot verify parental consent or determine every country’s requirements.

## 3. Accounts and security

Use a unique phone number that you control, keep your password private, and notify us promptly of suspected account compromise. Phone verification proves access to a number at a point in time; it does not prove civil identity or age. Do not share verification codes. We may apply reasonable rate limits and security checks. Account recovery, phone changes, and session revocation will use verification steps designed to prevent takeover.

You may deactivate or request deletion of your account in settings. We will explain any confirmation period, legal retention, safety hold, and backup deletion schedule before the live service launches.
